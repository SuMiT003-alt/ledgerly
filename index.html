      GST / THE TAX DECISION

      Same state or different state?
      That decides the tax ledger.

      Use the place of supply, not just the word “GST”. Try a scenario to see exactly when Tally needs CGST, SGST or IGST.

      01Choose the scenario
      Same state = split the tax.

      Use CGST for the Centre and SGST for the State. At 18%, each side is 9%.

      02Tally entry previewCGST + SGST
      Invoice / bill total₹11,800
      WHY?

      CGST and SGST are used because the supply stays within one state.

      ↔Intra-state

      Same state → CGST + SGST. Never add IGST to the same supply.

      →Inter-state

      Different state → IGST. Do not split it into CGST and SGST.

      ØExempt / nil-rated

      No GST ledger is posted. Record the sale or purchase at its actual value.
