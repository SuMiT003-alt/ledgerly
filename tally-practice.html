PRACTICE ROUTE

Build confidence in three passes.

01

Read before entering

Identify the business event, date, voucher type, source amount and ledgers before touching the form.

02

Use the worked example

Reveal the walkthrough when needed, then fill the entry and explain the narration in your own words.

03

Inspect the result

Use the Day Book and balance-sheet lab to see how one voucher changes the wider accounting picture.

Good practice: keep the training case separate from real business data. Ledgerly stores this exercise in the browser and does not connect to a Tally company or government portal.
COMMON QUESTIONS

Tally practice FAQs

Do I need Tally Prime installed?

No. Ledgerly is a browser learning simulation. It explains the voucher flow and standard shortcut references without opening or modifying a live Tally company.

Can I practise a complete month?

Yes. The fictional case contains 20 documents and connects the entry trainer to a live Day Book, trial balance and balance-sheet learning lab.

Can Document Coach post a voucher automatically?

Document Coach suggests a voucher and ledger mapping from a sample or uploaded document. You review and edit the suggestion before handing it into the training form.
