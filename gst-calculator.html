COMMON QUESTIONS

GST calculator FAQs

What is the difference between CGST and SGST?

For an intra-state practice scenario, the GST amount is split between Central GST and State GST. The exact applicability depends on the facts and current law.

When is IGST used?

IGST is used for inter-state supplies and other situations defined by GST rules. Confirm the place-of-supply treatment before relying on a calculation.

Does Ledgerly file a GST return?

No. Ledgerly prepares illustrative calculations and working papers. Portal login, payment and filing remain on the official GST portal or an authorised integration.
